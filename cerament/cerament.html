Biofilm

  • Bacteria encased in extracellular matrix
    • 1000x antibiotic resistance c.f. free-floating bacteria
  • Systemic administration limited by toxicity

  • Image credit: S. aureus biofilm, CDC/Janice Haney Carr

Cerament

  • 60% calcium sulfate
    • Antibiotic carrier
  • 40% hydroxyapatite
    • Osteoconductive

Cerament

  • Cerament G = G+ and G-, except MRSA (and resistant organisms); Saline
  • Cerament V = G+ only; Iohexol

Elution

  • Minimal Inhibitory Concentration (MIC) = Lowest concentration of an antibiotic to prevent visible growth in vitro
  • Minimal Biofilm Eradication Concentration (MBEC) = Lowest concentration of antibiotic to completely eradicate biofilm
  • PMMA = Most elution within 24 hours
